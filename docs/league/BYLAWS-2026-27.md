# Mr. PigSkin's: DEAD M🏈NEY OFFICIAL BYLAWS

## A GM-Style Dynasty Fantasy Football League

## Purpose & Philosophy

*Welcome y’all / mufuckas / y’all mufuckas\!\!\!*

Mr. PigSkin's: DEAD M🏈NEY is designed to simulate a real front-office experience. The league emphasizes long-term management of risk through contracts and salary cap management. In this format, Owners have the opportunity to act as General Managers (GMs) in the OFFSEASON, rookie and dispersal drafts, to go along with your not so typical fantasy SEASON (check out scoring\!).

## Sec. 2 — League Structure

**2.1 Membership:** 28 franchises divided into two (2) divisions of 14 teams each.
**2.2 Two-Copy System:** Each division has its own player pool. A real-life player may exist once in each division, but never twice on a single owner's teams.
**2.3 Multi-Team Ownership:** Owners may control one team per division. Trading between teams controlled by the same owner is strictly prohibited.
**2.4 Theme:** Each season a different theme will be picked. It helps provide stimulus so Owners can enjoy one of the BEST parts of redraft, coming up with fun ass names (pro tip, put your name in parentheses).

* Defense: 1 DL **\|** 1 LB **\|** 1 DB **\|** 2 ID Flex

**3.2 Bench & Minors:** 18 Reserve slots \+ 8 Minor League slots (add 2 every year to a cap of 16).

**3.4 The Yo-Yo Rule:** Players may move freely between Minors and Active rosters until they hit (20 NFL games played), they graduate and must be moved to the Active/Reserve roster (where their salary will count) or be released.

Any game delayed past Monday will count towards the week's scoring so long as it remains part of its originally scheduled league week (eg, a game that is delayed to Tuesday on Week 6 remains part of Week 6 scoring).

## Sec. 5 — FAAB & Waivers

**5.1 Allocation & Timing**: Each franchise is allocated \$200 FAAB (Free Agent Acquisition Budget) per season. Daily waivers process at 10:00 AM ET.

**5.2 Mid-Season FA Extension**: FA pickups are treated as 2nd-year players at the re-sign deadline if they are offered a contract extension. If the owner chooses not to extend the player, the player returns to the Free Agent pool at the conclusion of the season.

**5.3 The "Pure Free Agent" Exemption (FA Designation)**: Any player currently on the waiver wire with a "FA" contract designation (meaning they have not yet been signed to a fixed multi-year deal) may be picked up and dropped freely.

* No Cap Penalties: There are no financial penalties for dropping a "FA" designated player. This allows GMs to rotate depth and "stream" players without long-term financial consequences.

**5.4 Contracted Player Waivers & Penalties**: Any player currently under a fixed contract (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, or 6th-year designation) remains tied to their specific financial terms regardless of their waiver status.

* Cap Penalties Applied: If a GM drops a player under a fixed contract, the standard Cap Hit Penalties (as defined in the Section VIII Penalty Table) will be applied to the dropping team's salary cap for the remainder of the guaranteed term.
* Salary & Status Inheritance: Any new owner who claims a contracted player via waivers inherits the full salary and the exact contract year status of that player. The contract does not "reset" upon changing teams.
* Strategic Note: GMs should exercise extreme caution before dropping high-salary players in Years 1, 2, 4, or 5, as the resulting cap penalty can significantly hinder mid-season flexibility.

## Sec. 6 — Trades

**6.1 GIF Rule**: ALL trades must be announced in the league chat with a GIF.
**6.2 Inheritance**: No cap hits for trades; the receiving owner inherits the contract as-is.
**6.3 Approval & Timing**: All trades are contingent on commissioner approval. Trades are typically processed before games start. If a trade is accepted within 10 minutes of game-time, it may not be approved until the following day.
**6.4 GIFs & Integrity**: All trades must be announced with a GIF. While the league respects ownership rights, the Commissioner may intervene in cases of "catastrophic imbalance" (e.g., \$50 player for \$2 player) to protect league health.

## Sec. 7 — Startup Draft

### 7.1 Format & Scope

The inaugural DEAD M🏈NEY season begins with a 35-Round Slow Snake Draft. The draft will include the entire player pool (Veterans and Prospects).

### 7.2 Draft-Day Salary Cap Rules

To ensure a level playing field and realistic roster construction, the following rules apply ONLY during the Startup Draft:

* The Hard Cap is Live: Owners must stay under the Cap Limit (\$300,000,000) at all times. The Fantrax system will physically prevent any pick that puts a team over the cap.

* No Exemptions: During the draft, Minor League players count 100% against the salary cap. There are no stashes or exemptions until the draft is officially concluded.

* Strategic Responsibility: GMs are responsible for balancing "Star Power" with "Value Picks." If you spend \$300M in the first 10 rounds, you will run out of money, and wont be able to field a full roster.

**7.3 Post-Draft Transition (The "Unlock"):** Once the 35th round is complete, the Commissioner will update the league settings to activate Section III (Minor League Exemptions).

* At this point, any player moved into a designated Minor League Slot will have their salary removed from the Active Cap.

* This "Unlock" provides GMs with immediate cap relief and the flexibility to utilize the Yo-Yo Rule for the upcoming season.

**7.4 Illegal Rosters:** If a team is over the cap at any point after the draft (due to trades or clerical errors), their statistics will be frozen, and they will receive an automatic "Loss" for every matchup until the roster is compliant.

## Sec. 8 — Rookie Draft

**8.1 Timing:** The Rookie Draft occurs annually. It is a 5-round slow draft (3-4 hours per pick).
**8.2 Eligibility:** Incoming players who are deemed eligible rookies for the upcoming season.
**8.3 Rookie Salary Scale:**

* Pick 1: \$5M \| Pick 2: \$4.5M \| Pick 3: \$4M \| Pick 4: \$3.5M \| Pick 5: \$3M
* Pick 6: \$2.5M \| Pick 7: \$2M \| Pick 8: \$1.5M \| Pick 9: \$1M \| Pick 10-14: \$950k-\$750k
* Picks 15-60: \$600k down to \$300k.

**8.4 Signing Window:** Owners have a 4-week period after the draft to clear cap space and roster spots to sign rookies. Unsigned rookies enter the Free Agent Auction.

## Sec. 9 — Dispersal Draft

**9.1 Definition and Trigger**: A Dispersal Draft occurs during the offseason if one or more franchises become vacant due to an owner leaving, being removed for inactivity, or being expelled for integrity violations. It serves as the primary mechanism for integrating new owners and refreshing existing ones.
**9.2 The Asset Pool**: All assets from the vacant franchises are consolidated into a single "Dispersal Pool." This includes:

* The Full Roster: Every player, including their current Fantrax Salary and remaining Contract Years.
* Draft Capital: All Rookie Draft picks for the current year and all future years currently held by the vacated teams.

**9.3 Participant Eligibility**:
New Owners: Any owner entering the league to fill a vacancy must participate in the Dispersal Draft to build their initial roster.
Existing Owners (The "Fresh Start" Clause): At the end of each season, current owners will be asked if they intend to return. Any returning owner may opt to forfeit their entire roster and all draft picks to enter the Dispersal Draft.
Frequency Restriction: To prevent constant "churning," an existing owner is prohibited from joining a Dispersal Draft in two consecutive seasons.
**9.4 Confidentiality of Entry**: To protect the strategic integrity of the draft and prevent owners from "calculating" the quality of the pool before committing, current owners must notify the Commissioner of their intent to join the dispersal privately. The final list of available players and picks will not be revealed until all participants are locked in.
**9.5 Draft Mechanics**: The Dispersal Draft is a snake-style draft format. The order is determined by a random draw once the pool of participants is finalized.
Draft Length: Participants are not required to fill a maximum roster. A GM may choose to "Stop Drafting" at any point once they feel their core is established, opting to fill remaining spots via the Free Agent Auction or subsequent Rookie Draft.
Salary Cap Compliance: All players drafted carry their existing salaries. GMs are responsible for ensuring their drafted team remains under the \$500M Hard Cap.

**11.4 The Franchise Tag**: The Franchise Tag is a high-stakes "bridge" contract that allows a GM to retain an elite player for one additional season when a long-term extension isn't possible. Owners can "buy" up to one additional tag via the Reinvestment Tiers (\$15 and \$35 tiers), for a maximum of two tags per season.
**11.4.1 Eligibility**: The Tag can only be applied to players with expiring contracts (end of Year 3 or Year 6). A player cannot be tagged more than two years in a row.
**11.4.2 Salary Calculation**: A tagged player’s salary for the season is the GREATER of:

* 120% of the player's previous year's salary.
* The Average of the Top 5 highest-paid players at their specific position in the league.

  *Note: This prevents "cheating" by tagging elite \$300k rookies for pennies.*

  *If they perform like stars, you must pay them like stars.*

  **11.4.3 Guarantee & Penalty**: Franchise Tags are 100% fully guaranteed. If a tagged player is dropped at any point, the team incurs a 50% Cap Hit penalty for the remainder of that season.

**11.5 Salary Determination & Valuation**
A player’s salary is established at the moment they enter a roster and is managed via the Fantrax automated salary system.
**11.5.1 The Fantrax Algorithm**: Player valuations in Dead Money are driven by the Fantrax Valuation Engine. This proprietary algorithm calculates a player's worth based on:

* League Scoring System: Salaries are weighted specifically to Mr. PigSkin's: Dead Money Points settings.
* Projections: Forward-looking data for upcoming performance.
* Historical Stats: Past performance and reliability.
* Position Scarcity: Premium positions (like elite QBs in this league) will naturally command higher valuations.
  **11.5.2 The "New Value" Window (Year 2 Extension)**
* When a player reaches the extension window (after the trade deadline in Year 2), the "New Value" is not a guess—it is generated by the site.
* The Commissioner will trigger the "Recalculate Salaries" function on Fantrax.
* The system will run the new values based on the most up-to-date projections and performance data.
* Owners will see the exact price required to extend the player for Years 4–6.

**11.6 Contract Types & Updates**
**11.6.1 Market Fluctuations**: While Year 1–3 salaries are fixed (locked), the potential extension value displayed by Fantrax may fluctuate throughout the season based on the player’s hot or cold streaks. The new Salary could increase or decrease depending on how that player has performed.

## Sec. 12 — Player Contracts & Re-Signs

**12.1 Entry Contracts:** 3-year term. Base salary stays fixed for Years 1–3. Undrafted rookies and unsigned free agents will be assigned a default salary of \$2,000,000.

**12.2 Re-Valuation Window (*Year 2*):** After the trade deadline in Year 2, salaries for all Year 2 and (FA) players are updated based on production and projections. In Dead Money, the Year 2 Trade Deadline is the "Pencils Down" moment for franchise-altering contract decisions (resigns\!). While many leagues wait until a contract expires to offer extensions, Dead Money forces GMs to act a full year early (ie, at the trade deadline of the second year).

* **The Philosophy of the Year 2 Extension Window:** This rule is a cornerstone of the league’s "High-Stakes" philosophy. In a standard dynasty league, the "Year 3" decision is easy because you have all the data. In Dead Money, we move that window to the Year 2 Trade Deadline to create a pressure cooker for GMs. This timing is designed for four specific reasons:
  1. **Forced Risk Assessment (The "Crystal Ball" Factor)**: A lot can happen between the end of Year 2 and the end of Year 3\. A breakout star in Year 2 could suffer a catastrophic injury or a massive performance regression in Year 3\.
  2. **Big Stakes / Decisions**: By forcing the extension in Year 2, you have to decide if a player is a true cornerstone before you see their full "contract year" performance.
     * The Stakes: If you extend a player who falls off a cliff in Year 3, you are stuck with that "New Value" salary and a heavy cap penalty for Years 4-6. If you wait and they explode in Year 3, you lose them to the Free Agent pool.
  3. **Trade Deadline Maximum Leverage**: The Year 2 Trade Deadline becomes the most chaotic day on the Dead Money calendar.
     * Buyers vs. Sellers: Owners of expiring Year 2 stars who don't want to pay the "New Value" extension are able to move them to teams who want to secure resign rights.
     * The "Rental" Trap: Buyers must decide if they are trading for a 1-year rental (Year 3\) or if they are willing to inherit the "New Value" extension immediately. This prevents "super-teams" from easily stacking expiring contracts without taking on long-term financial risk.
  4. **Elimination of the "Wait and See" Strategy**: In Dead Money, procrastination is a competitive disadvantage. Most GMs want to wait until the last possible second to make a commitment. Our system removes that safety net.
     * You must have the conviction to identify your "Core" team while navigating the VERY REAL salary cap limitations.
* The GM’s Dilemma: "Do I lock in my budding ace at his projected \$25M extension price now, hoping he stays healthy through Year 3? Or do I let him walk, play out his cheap Year 3, and risk losing him to a \$45M bidding war in the Free Agent Auction?"

**12.3 Extension Decision:** Owners must decide to extend at the re-sign deadline. Drafted players who are not re-signed will remain on the team at their current salary through the end of Year 3 before entering the FA pool.

**12.4 Injury Clause:** Injured players are re-evaluated once healthy (Jan). Decisions must be made regardless of injury severity.

**12.5 Reset Rule**: A player’s contract designation ONLY resets if they finish an entire season in the Free Agent pool. Dropping and re-claiming a player mid-season does NOT reset their contract years.

## Sec. 13 — Postseason & Lotto

**13.1 Playoff Qualification**: At the conclusion of the 13-week regular season, the top ten (10) teams from each division (Division A and Division B) will qualify for the postseason based on the league's primary tie-breaking criteria.

**13.2 The Division Playoff Schedule**: The postseason consists of four internal rounds within each division to determine a Division Champion.

* **Week 13: The Wild Card Round**
  * **Seeds \#1 through \#6** receive a first-round **BYE**.
  * **Matchup 1:** Seed \#7 vs. Seed \#10
  * **Matchup 2:** Seed \#8 vs. Seed \#9
* **Week 14: Division Quarterfinals (The Elite 8\)**
  * The two Wild Card winners join **Seeds \#1 through \#6**.
  * **Logic:** Brackets will re-seed so the \#1 seed plays the lowest remaining seed.
* **Week 15: Division Semifinals (The Final 4\)**
  * The four remaining teams in each division compete.
  * **Logic:** Highest remaining seed plays the lowest remaining seed.
* **Week 16: Division Finals**
  * The final two teams in each division compete for the **Division Championship**.
* **Week 17: The MEGA Bowl**
  * The two Division Champions compete for the **MEGA Bowl Championship**.

**13.3 The MEGA & Lotto Bowls**: WELCOME TO THE SHIPs\!\!\!

The Champion of Riddell Division will face the Champion of Wilson Division in a winner-take-all matchup for the League Title.

## Sec. 15 — The Treasury & Reinvestment Distribution

**15.1 Mr. PigSkin's: Dead Money Treasury:** All revenue generated through the purchase of Optional Reinvestment Tiers (Salary Cap increases, Franchise Tags, and Amnesty Provisions) shall be deposited directly into a dedicated league account. This account is managed via the **Fantrax Treasurer** (or designated league site treasurer) and monitored by the Commissioner to ensure total financial transparency.

**15.2 Fund Integrity:** These funds are separate from initial entry fees and are held in trust throughout the duration of the season. The total "Reinvestment Pot" will be visible to all GMs to track the growing prize pool.

**15.3 Distribution of Funds:** At the conclusion of the MEGA Bowl, the entirety of the Reinvestment Pot will be redistributed to the top-performing franchises. To reward the elite tier of the league, the funds will be split between the two divisions and awarded to the **Top 6 Teams** in each division.

**15.4 Equal Share Payouts:** Unlike standard tiered payouts, the Dead Money Reinvestment Fund utilizes an **Equal Share Model.** The total pool is divided into **twelve (12) equal shares.**

* **Recipients:** The 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, and 6th place finishers in **each** division.
* **Calculation:** (Total Reinvestment Fund \\div 12\) \= Payout per Team.

**15.5 Disqualification:** Any team found to be in violation of Section 6.4 (Illegal Rosters) or Section 1.3 (Competitive Integrity) at the end of the season forfeits their right to a Reinvestment Share. Their share will be folded back into the pot and redistributed among the remaining eligible winners.

Why the "Equal Share" Model works for DEAD M🏈NEY:

* **The "Safety Net":** In a 28-team league, finishing 6th is a massive achievement. Giving them the same share as 1st place keeps the race for those final playoff spots incredibly intense until the final day.
* **Transparency:** By using the site’s official Treasurer, you remove any "friend of the commish" skepticism. Everyone sees the money go in, and everyone sees it go out.
